# General Tso's announcer recording

The six `announcer-*.mp3` clips are excerpts of **Boxing Announcer Montage – Dariusz Chmaj**, assembled by **unfa** from a real Polish boxing announcer recording and crowd/applause recordings.

- Original: https://freesound.org/people/unfa/sounds/210072/
- License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Changes: selected short passages, mono conversion, filtering, dynamics normalization, and fades.

The captions in General Tso's casino are fictional English *parody subtitles*, not translations or a quotation of the recording.

No text-to-speech, cloned voice, or political broadcast recordings are used.
