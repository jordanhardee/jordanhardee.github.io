✦ Patrick Stewart — In Memoriam

IN MEMORY · 1940–2026

Sir Patrick Stewart

An extraordinary actor, and the man who made Captain Jean-Luc Picard a lasting example of courage, curiosity, and decency.

From Shakespeare to the bridge of the Enterprise-D, Patrick Stewart brought intelligence and humanity to every role. For generations of viewers, Star Trek: The Next Generation showed that a hero could lead by listening, reasoning, and standing up for what was right.

He returned to that role decades later in Star Trek: Picard, giving us one more voyage with a character who meant so much to so many.
